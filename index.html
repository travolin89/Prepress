Ink behaviour

No file open.
Where two colours meet and misregistration could show paper, the lighter colour (lower neutral density) spreads into the darker one. Colours that already share enough ink, or differ by less than the step limit, are left alone. Traps are overprinting vector objects on the Traps layer; running again replaces them. Opaque inks (metallics) get the others spread under them; ignored inks (varnish, white) are left out.
